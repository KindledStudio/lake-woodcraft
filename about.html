Meet Nathaniel Lake

Crafted with Purpose. Built with Pride.

I'm Nathaniel Lake, the maker behind Lake Woodcraft. I've worked with wood for a long time, and lately I've become even more passionate about it.

I make pieces that are useful every day and nice to have around the holidays. What I enjoy most is finishing a project, and the best part is when something I made puts a smile on a customer's face.

Everything I make is handcrafted, durable, and built to last. It's not cheap, and it's not mass produced.

Want a certain color or size, or something custom? Just ask.
