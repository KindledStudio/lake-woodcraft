Meet the maker

Lake Woodcraft is a family woodworking business in Western New York. Every piece is made by hand, one at a time, from start to finish.

What started as a side business is growing, with new pieces added through the year and more places to find them.

When you buy a Lake Woodcraft piece, you are buying something made by a real person in a real shop, and built to last.
