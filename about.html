Meet Nathaniel Lake

Every piece starts as plain wood and ends up as something you'll keep.

I'm Nathaniel Lake, the maker behind Lake Woodcraft. I build every piece by hand, one at a time, in Western New York.

You'll see the grain, the stain, and the small differences that come from being made by hand. That's the point.
